Pointer / Gaze

Use the mouse or local webcam gaze tracking to explore one region at a time. Y selects notes inside the global mode, X controls stereo, movement adds energy, and boundaries create clicks.

The scan crosses the complete artwork over a duration you choose. Up to five regions can sound together.
