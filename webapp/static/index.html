Current interaction logic

Pointer / Gaze

Each region selects a track from the ten-minute master score. Pointer and gaze follow one shared timeline and reveal the active region track.

Left-to-Right Scan

The scan can last up to ten minutes. A five-minute scan plays 00:00–05:00 of the master score while the image activates up to five region tracks.
