# Local audio sample credits

These audio files are bundled so the web interface can perform without an internet connection.

## Pitched instruments

Files under `bass/`, `guitar/`, `strings/`, and `mallet/` are selected and renamed copies from [nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments).

- Collection: tonejs-instruments by Nicholaus P. Brosowsky and contributors
- Retrieved revision: `622c2f1c32c8cfce4158ddc3eb26e518ddef37e5`
- Sample license: [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
- Original sources catalogued upstream: Karoryfer Samples, University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios, and Versilian Studios / VSCO Community Edition
- Local mapping: Electric Bass (`bass-electric`), Acoustic Guitar (`guitar-acoustic`), Violin (`violin`), and Xylophone (`xylophone`)

The surrounding tonejs-instruments loader code is MIT licensed. This project uses its sample recordings through a small native Web Audio loader and does not include the upstream loader code.

## Drums and percussion

Files under `drums/` are selected and renamed copies from [octalmusic/octal-samples](https://github.com/octalmusic/octal-samples), pack `house-core`.

- Sample license: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Retrieved revision: `22d222ce36e290ba3792c776811aa9c69dcc8776`
- Local mapping: kick, snare, closed hi-hat, open hi-hat, clap, and tom

The upstream project documents per-file provenance and deterministic processing in its `LICENSES.md` and `manifest.json`.
